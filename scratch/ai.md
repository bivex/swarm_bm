# 🤖 ISO International Standards Roadmap for Artificial Intelligence (AI) & Machine Learning (ML)

> Official Committee: **ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 (Artificial Intelligence)**  
> Total Standards in Registry: **110 International Standards & Specifications**  
> Primary Classification: **ICS 35.020, 01.040.35**

---

## 🏛️ 1. AI Governance & Management Systems (AIMS)

| ISO Standard | Title | Key Governance Focus | Lifecycle Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🤖 **ISO/IEC 42001:2023** | *Artificial Intelligence — Management System (AIMS)* | **Flagship World Standard** for establishing, implementing, and continually improving AI management systems. | Published (Stage 6060) |
| 💼 **ISO/IEC 38507:2022** | *Governance implications of the use of AI by organizations* | Executive Board & C-level guidance for managing strategic AI risks and liabilities. | Published (Stage 6060) |
| ⚖️ **ISO/IEC 42005:2025** | *AI system impact assessment (AIIA)* | Structured methodology for assessing societal, ethical, privacy, and safety impacts of AI deployment. | Published (Stage 6060) |
| 🔍 **ISO/IEC 42006:2025** | *Requirements for bodies providing audit and certification of AIMS* | Accreditation and audit requirements for independent bodies certifying ISO 42001 compliance. | Published (Stage 6060) |

## 🧠 2. Machine Learning, LLM & Generative AI Frameworks

| ISO Standard | Title | Key Architecture & LLM Focus | Lifecycle Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⚙️ **ISO/IEC 23053:2022** | *Framework for Artificial Intelligence (AI) Systems Using Machine Learning (ML)* | Architectural framework for ML pipelines, MLOps, model training, feature stores, and inference. | Published (Stage 6060) |
| 📖 **ISO/IEC 22989:2022** | *AI Concepts and Terminology* (incl. **DAmd 1: Generative AI**) | Foundational terminology + official specifications for **Generative AI, LLMs, and Prompt Systems**. | Published (Stage 6060 / Amendment) |
| 👁️ **ISO/IEC 12792:2025** | *Transparency taxonomy of AI systems* | Classification framework for Explainable AI (XAI), algorithmic transparency, and model auditability. | Published (Stage 6060) |

---

## 🛡️ 3. AI Risk Management, Robustness & Quality

| ISO Standard | Title | Safety & Risk Focus | Lifecycle Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 📊 **ISO/IEC 23894:2023** | *Guidance on risk management for AI* | Specific AI risk management (mitigating model hallucinations, data bias, prompt injection, and drift). | Published (Stage 6060) |
| 🔬 **ISO/IEC 24029-2:2023** | *Assessment of the robustness of neural networks* | Formal mathematical verification methods for assessing deep neural network stability and safety. | Published (Stage 6060) |
| 🏅 **ISO/IEC 25059:2023** | *SQuaRE — Quality model for AI systems* | System quality model for AI (measuring autonomy, adaptability, fault tolerance, and learning quality). | Published (Stage 9092) |

---

## 🗃️ 4. Data Quality for ML & LLM Training (ISO/IEC 5259 Series)

| ISO Standard | Title | Training Data & Dataset Focus | Lifecycle Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 📊 **ISO/IEC 5259-1:2024** | *Data quality for analytics and ML — Part 1: Overview & Terminology* | Standardized terminology for dataset annotation, feature engineering, and data quality. | Published (Stage 6060) |
| 📏 **ISO/IEC 5259-2:2024** | *Data quality measures for analytics and ML* | Quantitative metrics for measuring dataset completeness, balance, accuracy, and noise ratio. | Published (Stage 6060) |
| 📋 **ISO/IEC 5259-3:2024** | *Data quality management requirements and guidelines* | Operational requirements for dataset curation, lineage tracking, and provenance logging. | Published (Stage 6060) |
| ⚙️ **ISO/IEC 5259-4:2024** | *Data quality process framework* | Standardized process workflows for cleaning, labeling, and validating training data. | Published (Stage 6060) |
| 🏛️ **ISO/IEC 5259-5:2025** | *Data quality governance framework* | Enterprise data governance framework for AI training datasets and synthetic data generation. | Published (Stage 6060) |
